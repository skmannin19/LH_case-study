Slide 03 · Engineering Triage (Weeks 1–4 / 2 Sprints)

Top 3 Asks for the Sales AI Engineer (Ranked by Evidence)

Capacity: 1 AI Engineer (Thomas) · 2 Sprints (4 Wks)
Engineering Collaboration Protocol: Thomas cannot build everything in 4 weeks. He works through these top 3 improvements directly alongside the respective pilot sellers who gave initial feedback, and those sellers co-test the solutions he designs.
Priority #1 · Foundation & Visibility

Fix Salesforce Activity Auto-Logging

Origin: Rep B ("Manager thinks I did nothing")
The Problem: Emails and calls sent in Maverick fail to sync to Salesforce Task history.
Why It Takes Precedence: If activity is invisible in Salesforce, managers cannot coach, reporting breaks, and trust is destroyed.
The Fix & Co-Testing: Bi-directional REST sync with `Outbound_Source__c = Maverick_Signal`. Rep B co-tests in Sprint 1 sandbox.
Priority #2 · Data Accuracy

Fix Stale Contacts at the Hotel Level

Origin: Rep G ("Half the contacts already left the hotel")
The Problem: High hospitality turnover leads to outdated General Managers and Directors of Sales.
Why It Takes Precedence: Benefits all sellers globally. Stale data slows reps down, causes bounces, and defeats faster prospecting.
The Fix & Co-Testing: Fresh data integration + 1-click LinkedIn Live Verification link. Rep G co-tests in Sprint 1/2.
Priority #3 · Global Adoption

Fix Multi-Language Support (Native DE / FR / ES / JP)

Origin: Reps D & E (DACH/France 24% send rate)
The Problem: Maverick generated English-only drafts, forcing EMEA and APAC sellers to abandon or manually translate.
Why It Takes Precedence: Reps D & E researched 120 hotels but sent only 23 emails due to language. Unlocks 60+ global sellers.
The Fix & Co-Testing: Target-language prompt toggle in prompt settings. Reps D & E co-test native German & French in Sprint 2.

Behavioral Issue: "Forgot where it was; went to spreadsheets" (Rep C)

Not a Product Bug

Root Cause: This is NOT an engineering or product bug. Rep C was specifically chosen for a high-priority pilot and failed to ask teammates, their manager, or the launch team for help. This is an accountability issue to be addressed directly with their frontline manager.

HOW WE VALIDATE & DEPLOY FIXES SAFELY
1

Feedback Aggregation & Solution Confirmation

Aggregate feedback into common problem buckets. Before handing off any ticket to Thomas, circle back to the originating reps to confirm: "Does this proposed design solve your exact workflow blocker?"

2

Tier 1 & Tier 2: Beta Testing Team Validation

For foundational or revenue-critical fixes (T1 & T2), pull together a focused beta testing group from the originating reps (e.g., Rep B for SFDC, Reps D/E for language) to validate in sandbox prior to general rollout.

Tier 3: Low-Touch Rollout & Team Call Awareness

For minor refinements (T3 nice-to-haves), deploy directly without beta cycles and announce in regional Slack channels and weekly team call agendas to maintain momentum without change fatigue.

Playbook for Skeptical Top Performers
1. Active Listening First: Do not argue. Listen deeply to understand their fear—often that AI drafts will sound generic or hurt their performance.
2. Shadow a Live Prospecting Block: Sit alongside them for 45 mins. Observe their manual research, take system improvement notes, and let them ask questions safely.
3. Escalation for Refusal (Behavioral Issue): If a rep refuses to shadow or attend support, that is a performance issue for their manager. Maverick is mandatory for standard prospecting.
Secret Post-Launch SPIFF (Month 1 Post-Launch) Unannounced

Reward top active sellers with an unannounced cash incentive 1 month post-launch. Keeping it secret from managers and sellers prevents gamified behavior and rewards genuine, habitual adoption.

WHY WE EXCLUDE "DAYS ACTIVE"

The pilot measured "days active" but never defined it—is it a dashboard login, moving a mouse, or clicking for X minutes? Because the metric is vague and easily gamed, we exclude it from leadership reporting and track concrete output metrics instead.

DAILY ANOMALY & GAMING TRACKING

GTM Productivity Specialist monitors the live dashboard daily. If a rep or team shows a sudden surge of 50+ actions on Friday afternoon or across a single hour, it is flagged as an anomaly in the weekly exec report for manager follow-up.

CORE ASSUMPTION & SEGMENTATION

All outreach metrics assume direct tracking inside Maverick. Furthermore, while the global reply rate rose from 3% to 7.45%, our weekly reports break this out by segment (Independent vs Groups) and region (AMER, EMEA, APAC).
